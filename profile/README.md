## What SocialGravity does

A supported workflow asks whether a buyer may use a named person's face or voice for an exact
request at that moment.

- The permission record binds the person, approved identity asset and signed terms.
- The check covers the buyer, purpose, campaign, format, channel, territory, timing and current
  status.
- Missing, expired, revoked or out-of-scope requests do not proceed through the supported route.
- A registered release can carry a receipt linking the output to the permission and protected
  operation.

## Permission chain

**Person → Permission → Production → Released file**

A positive decision covers the request that was checked. It is not permission for a different
buyer, campaign or use. A receipt covers the registered output it identifies, not every use on
the internet.

## Check the evidence

- [Try the synthetic receipt verifier](https://socialgravity.ai/resources/verify-a-receipt).
- [Read the public receipt specification](https://github.com/socialgravity/receipts/blob/main/docs/receipt-spec-v1.md).
- [Run the zero-dependency verifier](https://github.com/socialgravity/receipts).
- [Inspect the public ledger anchors](https://github.com/socialgravity/ledger-anchors).

The verifier reports `PASS`, `FAIL` or `NOT CHECKABLE` for each check. It does not turn an
assertion into proof when the evidence is unavailable.

## Product boundary

SocialGravity provides permission and evidence infrastructure. Talent discovery and generative
models remain separate. Nobody is listed, cloned or used through SocialGravity without explicit
consent. Each integration names the workflow and evidence it can actually control.
